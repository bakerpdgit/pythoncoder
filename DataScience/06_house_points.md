# Task: house points

`points` is a table: one **row** per house, one **column** per week.

Replace each `____`:

| Line | Should hold | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `house_totals` | each house's total over the five weeks | 4 numbers, one per house |
| `best_each_week` | the highest score in each week | 5 numbers, one per week |
| `week_3` | every house's points in Week 3 | 4 numbers - a column |

Remember the rule from page 5: **`axis` names the direction that disappears**. To
get one number per house, the weeks have to disappear. And Week 3 is column
number **2**, because counting starts at 0.

## How it is marked

The printed totals, the best score each week, Week 3's column and the winner are
checked against the right answers.
