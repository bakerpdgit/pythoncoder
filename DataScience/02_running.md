# Task: a week of running

A runner logged seven days of distances in kilometres, alongside the distances
their plan said to run. Replace each `____` with **one line** of NumPy.

| Line | Should hold |
| --- | --- |
| `miles` | every distance in miles: 1 km is 0.621371 miles |
| `difference` | how far each run was over (+) or under (-) the plan |
| `total` | the total distance for the week, in km |

Everything is already in arrays, so none of these needs a loop - each is one
calculation on whole arrays, as on page 1.

Press **Run** to check your answers look sensible, then **Submit**.

## How it is marked

The printed lines are checked against the right answers, so do not change the
`print` lines. Your code must not contain a `for` loop: the point of the page is
that the arrays do the looping for you.
