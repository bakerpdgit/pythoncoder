# Task: favourite subjects

A year group voted for their favourite subject. Draw the result as a chart worth
reading:

![preview](chartpreview)

| Part | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Colours | `"#16a34a"` (green) for the subject with the most votes, `"#bbf7d0"` (pale green) for the rest |
| Bars | horizontal, one per subject, coloured with that list |
| Order | the first subject (Maths) at the top: flip the y axis |
| Title | `Favourite subject` |
| x axis | `Votes` |

The colour list is half written: it is the same list comprehension as on page 21.

## How it is marked

Your chart is compared with the expected one: the bars' subjects and values, which
way up they are, the title and the axis label - and, on this page, the **colour**
of every bar.
