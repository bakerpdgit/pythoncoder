# Task: a school survey

150 students answered a survey about sleep, screens, exercise and getting to school.
Their answers are in `data/survey.csv` - open it in the file browser to see it.

Replace each `____` with one line of pandas:

| Line | Should hold |
| --- | --- |
| `survey` | the CSV file read into a DataFrame |
| `students` | how many students answered: the number of rows |
| `mean_sleep` | the average of the `hours_sleep` column |
| `longest` | the largest value in the `minutes_to_school` column |

`len()` works on a DataFrame and gives its number of rows.

## How it is marked

The printed column names and the three answers are checked against the right
answers.
