# Task: questions for the survey

Three questions about the survey, each answered by choosing or ordering rows.

Replace each `____`:

| Line | Should hold |
| --- | --- |
| `short_sleepers` | only the students with `hours_sleep` **less than 8** |
| `quick_cyclists` | only the students whose `travel_mode` is `"Cycle"` **and** whose `minutes_to_school` is less than 15 |
| `by_screen` | every student, sorted by `screen_time` with the **most** first |

For `quick_cyclists` the brackets and `&` are already there: write one condition in
each pair of brackets. Text is compared with `==`, just like numbers.

## How it is marked

The counts, the mean screen time of the short sleepers and the top three by screen
time are checked against the right answers.
